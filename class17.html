Bangladesh

Bangladesh is a country in South Asia. It is the eighth-most populous country in the world, with a population exceeding 160 million people The capital and largest city is Dhaka. Bangladesh is known for its rich cultural heritage, diverse landscapes, and vibrant traditions. The country has a parliamentary democracy and has made significant progress in various sectors, including education, healthcare, and economic development. Despite facing challenges such as natural disasters and political instability, Bangladesh continues to strive for growth and prosperity.
